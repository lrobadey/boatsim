Model assumptions

Mass follows hull displacement. CG, inertia, long-oar parameters, windage and rudders need calibration. Three/five hands per oar scale the existing motor and physiology as a group; individual grips are unresolved. Rowers are inside the hulls. Tow sizing uses estimated resistance: no catamaran interference, additional appendage form drag or structural loads. Paddle input assumes 60% shaft-to-hull efficiency; human sizing assumes 140 W at 40–60%. The 180 s ramp excludes added mass. Test drive applies equal forward forces at both sterns, without a paddle mechanism. Calm water, 100 m seabed depth; no sailing world or interior collision.

Force arrows: water + weight (cyan), oars (gold), sails (cream), wind (blue), rudders (orange), test drive (green). 1 m = 1.5 kN, capped at 45 m.
