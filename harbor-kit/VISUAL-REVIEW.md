# Ormos asset-by-asset visual review

Inspected individually in the browser, improved where the differences mattered, rebuilt, then loaded again from the production bundle. These are studio views of the reusable assets, not game integration screenshots.

## amphora-large

Improved: continuous curved handles and continuous body UVs. The rounded silhouette and restrained clay color work well for close cargo views.

![amphora-large](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/amphora-large.jpg)

## amphora-small

Uses the repaired pottery construction at a smaller scale. Reuse with instancing for groups rather than copying geometry.

![amphora-small](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/amphora-small.jpg)

## cargo-boat

Preserved the silhouette the user liked. Layered hull planks, frames, cargo and rigging make this the strongest focal asset. It remains a scenic vessel, separate from playable-ship physics.

![cargo-boat](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/cargo-boat.jpg)

## coastal-shrub

Improved: denser clusters of small rounded leaf blades replace conspicuous triangular cards. Use around rock feet and ground transitions.

![coastal-shrub](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/coastal-shrub.jpg)

## gangplank

Readable plank divisions, traction cleats and rope rails. Its fixed 3.6 m span and endpoint anchors make it useful in placement logic.

![gangplank](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/gangplank.jpg)

## house-courtyard

Added returning stone plinth and localized corner repairs. The quiet limewashed shell works as a background building; the name denotes a house type, not an assembled courtyard.

![house-courtyard](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/house-courtyard.jpg)

## house-tower

Improved: elevated windows and an intermediate structural timber course give the tall facade a clearer scale.

![house-tower](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/house-tower.jpg)

## house-workshop

Improved: stronger curved tile relief and edge treatment. This smaller roof shows the individual tiles most clearly.

![house-workshop](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/house-workshop.jpg)

## linen-canopy

Folded cloth, sewn divisions, braces and posts are readable. Rear attachment anchors must meet a building; this is not a freestanding tent.

![linen-canopy](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/linen-canopy.jpg)

## mooring-stone

Improved: rounded profile transitions remove the faceted chess-piece appearance while retaining a clear neck for rope attachment.

![mooring-stone](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/mooring-stone.jpg)

## olive-a

Improved: fluted tapered trunk, low buttress roots and narrow folded leaves replace stilt roots and crossed cards. Instance for groves; avoid identical rotations.

![olive-a](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/olive-a.jpg)

## olive-b

Shares the improved tree construction with a different deterministic branching arrangement and scale.

![olive-b](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/olive-b.jpg)

## packing-rack

The open shelves provide practical anchor-driven cargo placement. Leave it separate from cargo for reuse.

![packing-rack](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/packing-rack.jpg)

## quay-corner

Two finished masonry faces let straight runs turn. The irregular joints help break repetition; inspect rotated joins in the eventual game layout.

![quay-corner](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/quay-corner.jpg)

## quay-straight-a

Clear coping, masonry courses and timber rub rail. Nominal 6 m connectors are verified independently of surface irregularity.

![quay-straight-a](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/quay-straight-a.jpg)

## quay-straight-b

Related masonry variant with secondary timber details. Mix with the other runs to reduce obvious repetition.

## quay-straight-c

![quay-straight-c](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/quay-straight-c.jpg)

## retaining-wall

A compact low masonry module with end connectors. Best used partially bedded into terrain.

![retaining-wall](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/retaining-wall.jpg)

## rock-boulder

Improved: proportionate shear, denser fractured surface and matching tessellation along shared edges remove the stretched silhouette and hairline cracks.

Improved: broader coherent fracture planes replace noisy sharp triangles. Final close-up recheck found no prior face seams.

Improved: compact proportions and added surface shape. Mix scale/rotation carefully; this is one reusable stone, not a rubble scatter.

![rock-rubble](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/rock-rubble.jpg)

![rope-coil](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/rope-coil.jpg)

## sea-stairs

## timber-beam

A deliberately simple structural component with longitudinal grain and end connectors.

![timber-beam](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/timber-beam.jpg)

## timber-bench

Improved: divided seat planks, visible pegs, leg shoulders and a stretcher replace the generic three-block bench.

![timber-bench](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/timber-bench.jpg)

Improved: deeper roof tile relief, returning stone footing and corner repairs. The interior contains racks and cargo; it is the heaviest building and should get a dedicated LOD before large-scale repetition.

![warehouse](/Users/lucarobadey/Developer/boatsim/harbor-kit/evidence/browser/warehouse.jpg)

## wicker-basket

Improved: hollow interior, woven rim, handles and alternating weave replace the solid top. This is a much more useful close-up cargo prop.
