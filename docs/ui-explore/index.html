What should the ultimate trireme simulator feel like on screen?

Four deliberately different directions, each pushed further than we'd ship, mocked over real frames from the game. Then one converged system that takes the best part of each, with tokens, components and screens. Questions for you are at the end.

What's wrong with the HUD today. Each panel was styled on its own: parchment minimap, bronze astrolabe, floating serif text, a frosted dock card. Three serif families do every job, so data and names look the same. There is permanent chrome (the ΤΡΙΗΡΗΣ corner mark, the controls hint) that you only need once. Nothing is broken, but nothing feels designed as one thing.

Converged: Wine-dark instrument

Film-like restraint while you sail (03), exact modern data (02), one brand colour and one brand mark (01), one instrument with character (04).

Principle 1

The sea is the screen. While sailing, nothing has a background. Panels appear only for tasks that need them (docking, building, map, pause) and leave when the task ends.

Principle 2

Names are serif, numbers are sans. Places, ratings and titles use Cormorant. Every number and label uses Inter Tight with tabular figures. Nothing else.

Principle 3

Colour means something. Bone for text. Terracotta for "this is active / yours". Bronze for steering and progress. Green, amber, red only for state.

Rules

TopicRule
Layout24 px safe margin (scales with viewport). Bottom left: ship state. Top centre: heading. Top right: map and time. Bottom centre: helm, then task panels above it. Centre: crosshair and one prompt line.
PresenceElements fade out when they stop changing: time and wind after 6 s of steady state, the order ladder after 4 s at a steady order, prompts once used three times. Anything changing fades back in.
MotionIn 180 ms ease-out with a 4 px rise, out 240 ms. Numbers never animate their digits. Moments: 600 ms fade and rise, hold 4 s, 900 ms out.
PanelsOne surface: night glass, 10 px radius, 1 px bone hairline at 11%, soft shadow. Never stacked, never more than one at a time beside the map.
KeycapsInter Tight 600, bone outline with a heavier bottom edge. Shown next to the thing they operate, never in a list while playing.
TextSentence case, plain words, units always. Caps only for small labels. No italics in the HUD.
LegibilityText on the image gets a soft 12 px shadow at 35%. Minimum 13 px at 1440 wide. Contrast checked over sky, sea glare and deck.

Questions for you

  1. How minimal while sailing? The converged version hides time, wind and the order ladder when they're steady. Do you want a "full instruments" toggle (H cycles: full → minimal → none), or minimal only?
  2. The astrolabe. It's the most distinctive piece today but it doesn't fit a modern system. Move it into the map screen (my recommendation), keep it in the HUD restyled, or drop it?
  3. Brand colour. Terracotta (red-figure pottery) is my pick. The alternative is bronze-gold as the single accent, which is safer but less ownable.
  4. Greek letters. Wordmark only (recommended), or also small Greek labels in places like the order names (e.g. "Half" with ΗΜΙ beneath)?
